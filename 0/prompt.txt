Make sure comp / field is used correctly in the diagnostics and operations for the changes in this PR. Particularly in the case of evaluate. Instead of being "physical field" in the descriptions please change "Physical" (and maybe add something about this in a skill file). Agents (and humans) like to refer to some things as physical (e.g. a vector component, Cartesian coords), but their counterparts are often physical too (e.g. a DG component, curvilinear coordinates); they just simply have another representation. So it's generally best to avoid this word.
