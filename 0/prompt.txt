› Why can't we just get rid of operations/plot.py?


• We could remove it, but its responsibilities must move somewhere:

  - Materialize point-value datasets and reject modal coefficients.
  - Group multiblock datasets into figures.
  - Generate indexed save paths.
  - Provide the CLI-compatible signature and command metadata.
  - Keep GData.plot() independent of Matplotlib internals.

  Moving those into render.matplotlib.plot would make the backend own application policy and require new gdata →
  render and render → dg dependencies. Its Python-only parameters (fig, axes offsets, figure objects) also cannot
  become CLI options cleanly.

  So the cleaner consolidation is not removing operations.plot; it is making it the sole public function and
  turning render.matplotlib.plot into a private drawing routine. The current mistake is exposing both publicly,
  especially pg.plot = render.plot.
